# AFTER THE RAIN

A small 3D paper-boat journey through a Malaysian shophouse courtyard after a tropical shower.

Guide with WASD/arrows or a gamepad left stick. Hold Space/south face button for a breeze. Release to coast. Find and gently nudge the companion with matching pencil stars; keep drifting or begin again.

Built in Godot4.6.3, GDScript and Compatibility, for single-threaded desktop-browser play. Original Blender meshes, patterned materials, offline music and sound sources are included.
