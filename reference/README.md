# Visual references

## Concept image

`after-the-rain-concept.png` is the original generated concept art from the design discussion, copied unchanged for this handoff.

Use it for the courtyard's mood, palette, materials, scale and framing. It is not a gameplay screenshot or a promise that the browser renderer will reproduce every reflection. The boat, environment, texture maps, lighting and effects still need to be built.

The concept image has not been used as a texture atlas, skybox, billboard world or substitute for interactive 3D geometry.

## Courtyard layout

`courtyard-layout.svg` and `courtyard-layout.png` are a top-down schematic based on the route in docs/GAME_DESIGN.md. They provide a useful first blockout, not final collision geometry or a mandatory camera path.

Follow the metre-based design coordinates and fixed water plane; tune clearance, current strengths and timing after trying the real controller. Preserve the main route, the two reconnecting detours, the destination sightline and the final reveal.

## Shipping

Keep these references available to the development team and exclude them from the game's exported pack. All production models and textures belong under assets/ with their own provenance.
